Standard PCR Amplification (Taq Polymerase, 50 uL Reaction)

Materials
- Taq 2X Master Mix, New England Biolabs, cat. M0270L
- Forward primer, 10 uM
- Reverse primer, 10 uM
- Nuclease-free water, Invitrogen, cat. AM9937
- Template genomic DNA
- 0.2 mL PCR tubes
- Thermal cycler
- 1% agarose gel with SYBR Safe, and 1 kb Plus DNA ladder

Reaction setup (on ice)
1. Thaw master mix, primers and template on ice. Vortex primers briefly and spin down.
2. For each reaction combine: 25 uL Taq 2X Master Mix, 1 uL forward primer, 1 uL reverse primer, template DNA (up to 1 ug), and nuclease-free water to 50 uL.
3. Include a no-template control that replaces the template with water.
4. Mix gently by pipetting and spin down. Record the template DNA amount added (ng).

Thermal cycling
5. Initial denaturation at 95 C for 30 seconds.
6. Run 30 cycles of: 95 C for 30 seconds, annealing at 58 C for 30 seconds, extension at 68 C for 1 minute per kb.
7. Final extension at 68 C for 5 minutes, then hold at 4 C.

Analysis
8. Run 5 uL of each product on a 1% agarose gel at 100 V for 40 minutes alongside the ladder.
9. Image the gel and confirm a single band of the expected size in sample lanes and no band in the no-template control.
10. Record the observed product size in bp.
