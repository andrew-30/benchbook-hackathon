BCA Protein Assay (Microplate Procedure)

Purpose: Determine total protein concentration of cell lysates before SDS-PAGE loading.

Materials
- Pierce BCA Protein Assay Kit (Reagent A and Reagent B), Thermo Fisher, cat. 23225
- Bovine serum albumin (BSA) standard, 2 mg/mL, Thermo Fisher, cat. 23209
- RIPA lysis buffer, Sigma-Aldrich, cat. R0278
- 96-well clear flat-bottom microplate
- Multichannel pipette (20-200 uL)
- Plate reader capable of reading absorbance at 562 nm
- Incubator set to 37 C

Section 1: Prepare standards
1. Thaw the BSA standard on ice and vortex briefly.
2. Prepare a dilution series of BSA in RIPA buffer at 2000, 1500, 1000, 750, 500, 250, 125, 25 and 0 ug/mL. Final volume of each standard should be 100 uL.
3. Keep standards on ice until use.

Section 2: Prepare working reagent
4. Calculate the volume of working reagent required: (number of standards + number of samples) x 2 replicates x 200 uL, plus 10% extra.
5. Mix Reagent A and Reagent B at a 50:1 ratio. The solution should turn clear green. Record the total volume prepared (mL).

Section 3: Run the assay
6. Pipette 25 uL of each standard and each unknown sample into the plate in duplicate.
7. Add 200 uL of working reagent to every well using the multichannel pipette.
8. Cover the plate and mix on a plate shaker for 30 seconds.
9. Incubate the plate at 37 C for 30 minutes.
10. Cool the plate to room temperature for 5 minutes.
11. Read absorbance at 562 nm. Record the blank-corrected absorbance of the 2000 ug/mL standard (expected range 1.8 - 2.4).
12. Fit a linear standard curve and record the R-squared value (acceptable if 0.98 or higher).
13. Calculate sample concentrations from the curve and record the mean sample concentration in ug/mL.
